## What this covers

This file is the map of what an Ads API seller can buy, who may buy it, what may be advertised, what an ad may say, how Amazon reviews it, and how it is paid for. It covers:

- the ad products an Ads API caller reaches: Sponsored Products (SP), Sponsored Brands (SB) in its formats, Sponsored Display (now called "Display" in the help center), Sponsored TV (STV, "Streaming TV" in the help center), Brand Stores, Posts and Amazon Live as far as they touch the API, and the 2025–2026 additions (Review Requests, Full-Funnel Campaigns, Sponsored Services, prompts, Amazon Business and Amazon Haul site restrictions);
- availability by marketplace and by account type: seller, vendor, KDP author, agency, non-Amazon seller;
- Brand Registry as the gate for SB, Display, STV and Stores;
- product eligibility: categories, condition, adult, Featured Offer, variation parents, suppressed listings, and the two eligibility APIs (per product and per program);
- ad policy: the structure of the policy tree, prohibited content and products, restricted categories, claims and substantiation, pricing and promotions, event-day wording, trademarks and Amazon's own brand, language and translation, AI-generated people;
- moderation: review times, statuses, rejection codes, appeals, the pre-moderation API and the moderation-results API;
- ads on AI shopping surfaces as of 2026: Alexa for Shopping (the US assistant into which Amazon brought Rufus and Alexa+ in May 2026), sponsored ads and prompts inside it, Rufus outside the US, Alexa+ Conversational Ads on Alexa-enabled devices, and Branded Conversations (§S);
- creators beside the ad products: Creator Connections (the bonus-commission marketplace, billed on the ads invoice but reported in its own portal), the Amazon Influencer and Associates Programs and creator storefronts as surfaces, and the Sponsored Products creator placements that began on 2026-08-10, told apart (§V);
- billing basics: what a click costs and when it is billed, payment methods per account type, credit limits, regulatory fees, promotional credits, and the billing API reads.

DSP is out of scope. In 2026 "Amazon Ads Agent" is the name of the unified platform that joined the ads console and Amazon DSP, holding three campaign types (sponsored ads, Display, Video, and Audio, and Full-Funnel Campaigns); DSP buying is now "Display, Video, and Audio campaigns" inside it, and the help center's phrase "Amazon Ads Agent (formerly known as Amazon DSP)" is that platform name put where its pages used to say "Amazon DSP" (§U). This file says where the DVA side begins (§U) and stops there.

## Amazon's own rules and guidance

Each item is marked RULE (Amazon states it as a requirement, a mechanism or a documented API behaviour) or GUIDANCE (Amazon advises it).

### A. The ad product map in 2026

- RULE. The Ads API v1 common model names the ad products as an enum `AdProduct`: `AMAZON_DSP`, `FULL_FUNNEL`, `RMS` ("Sponsored ads for external retailers"), `SPONSORED_BRANDS`, `SPONSORED_DISPLAY`, `SPONSORED_PRODUCTS`, `SPONSORED_TELEVISION`. The ad types are `AUDIO`, `COMPONENT`, `DISPLAY`, `PRODUCT_AD`, `THIRD_PARTY`, `VIDEO`. Cost types are `CPC`, `CPM`, `FIXED_PRICE`, `VCPM`. Goals are `AWARENESS`, `CONSIDERATION`, `CONVERSIONS`, `ENGAGEMENT`; the last is described as "customer engagement actions beyond product purchase, such as review requests or follows. Used by Sponsored Actions campaigns". [official] (Amazon Ads API v1 merged contract, https://d1y2lf8k3vrkfu.cloudfront.net/openapi/en-us/dest/AmazonAdsAPIALLMerged_prod_3p.json). The older "Enums" page of the common models (https://advertising.amazon.com/API/docs/en-us/reference/common-models/enums) still lists only `SPONSORED_PRODUCTS`, `SPONSORED_BRANDS` and `SPONSORED_DISPLAY` as `adProduct` and `PRODUCT_AD`, `IMAGE`, `VIDEO`, `PRODUCT_COLLECTION`, `STORE_SPOTLIGHT` as `adType`; the v1 values above come from the merged contract only. [official]
- RULE. A v1 campaign can carry `siteRestrictions` (at most one value): `AMAZON_BUSINESS` or `AMAZON_HAUL`, and `marketplaceScope` `GLOBAL` or `SINGLE_MARKETPLACE`. [official] (v1 merged contract, above; Campaign entity guide, https://advertising.amazon.com/API/docs/en-us/guides/campaign-management/entities/campaign)
- RULE. Amazon Ads Agent is the platform, not only the DSP: "Today at unBoxed 2026, we announced Amazon Ads Agent—the new name of the unified platform, now powered by agentic AI", which "brings three campaign types—sponsored ads, Full-Funnel Campaigns, and Display, Video, and Audio—together" [official] (Introducing Amazon Ads Agent, your AI-powered ad platform, https://advertising.amazon.com/library/news/amazon-ads-agent, datePublished 2026-09-29); "Amazon Ads Agent is the unified buying experience that brings together what was formerly the DSP and Ads Console" [official] (The 11 biggest takeaways from unBoxed 2026, https://advertising.amazon.com/library/news/unboxed-2026-news-announcements); the site navigation on every Amazon Ads page read on 2026-11-09 answers "What is Amazon Ads Agent?" with "We made it simpler to advertise with Amazon Ads by bringing together the ads console and Amazon DSP into a single buying surface we call Amazon Ads Agent." [official] (Ads Agent: An AI marketing assistant from Amazon Ads, https://advertising.amazon.com/solutions/products/ads-agent). The conversational experience inside it is a separate thing and is the part in closed beta: "This experience is in closed beta and continues to actively evolve." [official] (same product page). The help center writes the platform name where its older text said "Amazon DSP": "Amazon Ads Agent (formerly known as Amazon DSP)", in the same markup pattern as "ad (formerly known as Creative on Amazon DSP)" and "ad group (formerly known as Line Item on Amazon DSP)" on the ad review page [official] (Understand Advertiser Accounts, https://advertising.amazon.com/help/GXKXR56ZCMJ3WCS4, lastModified 2026-10-05; Learn about our Ad review process, https://advertising.amazon.com/help/G5BXPGD7CAVBMHPY, lastModified 2026-08-11); the Featured Offer page uses the same name for where a Sponsored Products campaign shows "Delivering" (§D). That the help-center phrase names the same platform, renamed term by term, is this seat's reading of the markup [seat]. And an "Advertiser Account (AA)" lets one account "create and manage campaigns using all ad-products and formats globally". A new Advertiser Account pays "by invoice for all ad campaigns under that account including sponsored ads", with one invoice for Display, Video, and Audio campaigns and a separate one for sponsored ads. [official] (Understand Advertiser Accounts, https://advertising.amazon.com/help/GXKXR56ZCMJ3WCS4)
- RULE. Sponsored Products is cost per click: "you only pay when a customer clicks your ad, and you're never billed more than once per click". [official] (Understand campaign spend, https://advertising.amazon.com/help/GUJY2CG5DTCPDCVQ)
- RULE. Sponsored Brands is bought by CPC, by vCPM, or as a fixed upfront "reserve share of voice" with a minimum spend commitment; it is reviewed "within 72 hours" (product page) [official] (Sponsored Brands | Amazon Ads, https://advertising.amazon.com/solutions/products/sponsored-brands). The help center says SB is "only available to vendors and professional sellers enrolled in the Amazon Brand Registry" and lists three formats (collections, store spotlight, video) [official] (Understand Sponsored Brands, https://advertising.amazon.com/help/GGWFYHL27MFXLHS6, lastModified 2026-10-20).
- RULE. Display (Sponsored Display) bills by CPC or vCPM and serves on and off Amazon; it requires a vendor or a Professional seller with Brand Registry. [official] (Understand Display, https://advertising.amazon.com/help/GHYXY6HJRDM5VCCY). In the API the two tactics are `T00020` (contextual targeting, sellers and vendors) and `T00030` (audiences, sellers, vendors and non-Amazon sellers) [official] (Sponsored Display overview, https://advertising.amazon.com/API/docs/en-us/guides/sponsored-display/overview; SD 3.0 contract, https://d3a0d0y2hgofx6.cloudfront.net/openapi/en-us/sponsored-display/3-0/openapi.yaml).
- RULE. The Sponsored Display product page now reads "Sponsored Display is now known as display ads" and "Sponsored Display is now a part of Amazon's wider display ads offering within our ad platform, Amazon Ads Agent"; "Existing Sponsored Display campaigns will continue to run uninterrupted. You can access your existing Sponsored Display campaigns through Display, Video, and Audio campaigns in Amazon Ads Agent", and new display campaigns are created under "Display, Video, and Audio" → "Display". The API guides and `/sd/` contract are still published on 2026-11-09, and no deprecation notice for the `/sd/` endpoints appears in the Deprecations page read. [official] (Sponsored Display – Create display advertising campaigns, https://advertising.amazon.com/solutions/products/sponsored-display; Deprecations, below)
- RULE. Sponsored TV serves on Prime Video, Twitch, live sports and Fire TV Channels as nonskippable video, with QR overlays [official] (Understand Streaming TV, https://advertising.amazon.com/help/GXFV9PSQ4356SNUF). The API guide calls it an open beta in BR, CA, MX, UK and US [official] (Sponsored TV overview, https://advertising.amazon.com/API/docs/en-us/guides/sponsored-tv/overview); the STV contract's currency table marks only US/USD as "Supported" and CA, MX and BR as "Coming Soon" [official] (Sponsored TV contract, https://d1y2lf8k3vrkfu.cloudfront.net/openapi/en-us/dest/SponsoredTV_prod_3p.json).
- RULE. unBoxed 2026 ("September 28-30 at the Moscone Center in San Francisco", unBoxed event page, https://advertising.amazon.com/events/unboxed) announced Full-Funnel Campaigns in open beta for "any US advertiser who sells on Amazon": one campaign across sponsored ads, display, video and STV. Amazon claims "67% higher Long-Term ROAS*" and 29% lower new-to-brand cost per purchase, citing its own June–August 2026 data — figures for human readers only. [official] (Grow total sales with Full-Funnel Campaigns, https://advertising.amazon.com/resources/whats-new/full-funnel-campaigns-from-discovery-to-sales, datePublished 2026-09-29)
- RULE. Sponsored Services is "for local and online service providers", bought with "CPO (Cost Per Outcome) bidding" (outcomes such as quote requests, calls and food orders), US only, "Available exclusively via Amazon Ads Unified API in Q4 2026. Ad Console access will follow in 2027", serving across "the Amazon Stores, Alexa+, and other premium surfaces". [official] (Sponsored Services: Capture demand now, https://advertising.amazon.com/resources/whats-new/sponsored-services-capture-demand-now, datePublished 2026-09-29; Understand Sponsored Services, https://advertising.amazon.com/help/G9VRPKVUGG4ER5SF)

### B. Who may advertise what: account types and marketplaces

- RULE. A profile's `accountInfo.type` is `vendor`, `seller` or `agency`, and `accountInfo.subType` may be `KDP_AUTHOR` or `AMAZON_ATTRIBUTION`. The profile carries `validPaymentMethod`, which the contract describes as "Only present for Vendors"; the list call filters by `profileTypeFilter` and `validPaymentMethodFilter`. "Seller accounts ... for Sponsored Brands and Sponsored Display, the account must be enrolled in the Brand Registry"; agency accounts are for DSP and data providers. A caller sees at most 5,000 profiles, and profiles are region-scoped (NA, EU, FE hosts). [official] (Profiles contract, https://d3a0d0y2hgofx6.cloudfront.net/openapi/en-us/profiles/3-0/openapi.yaml; Profiles guide, https://advertising.amazon.com/API/docs/en-us/guides/account-management/authorization/profiles)
- RULE. The API overview's host table lists the marketplaces per region: NA — US, CA, MX, BR; EU — UK, FR, IT, ES, DE, NL, AE, PL, TR, EG, SA, SE, BE, IN, ZA; FE — JP, AU, SG. [official] (API overview, https://advertising.amazon.com/API/docs/en-us/reference/api-overview). Ireland is not in that table, but the release note of 2025-06-27 reads "Sponsored ads and Stores launch in Ireland ... Sponsored Products, Sponsored Brands and Stores on Amazon.ie and via the Amazon Ads API", and the v1 `Marketplace` enum includes `IE`, `CL`, `CO` and `NG`. [official] (Release notes index, https://advertising.amazon.com/API/docs/en-us/release-notes/index; v1 merged contract, above)
- RULE. SP is "available for professional sellers, vendors, book vendors, Kindle Direct Publishing (KDP) authors, and agencies. Products must be in one or more eligible categories and be eligible for the Featured Offer in order to advertise." [official] (Sponsored Products | Amazon Ads, https://advertising.amazon.com/solutions/products/sponsored-products)
- RULE. The SP eligibility page lists the account conditions: an account in good standing, able to ship to the country, a valid payment method, "Professional seller, vendor, or author", and Featured Offer eligibility in at least one category. [official] (Sponsored Products eligibility requirements, https://advertising.amazon.com/help/GMJYF5GN8RNYB6HD, lastModified 2026-10-20). The API guide repeats that SP "is available for vendors, sellers and KDP authors" and its feature table covers every marketplace in the host table, including ZA. [official] (Sponsored Products overview and features, https://advertising.amazon.com/API/docs/en-us/guides/sponsored-products/overview, .../features)
- RULE. SB: an active Professional Seller account or an approved Vendor; "Sellers must be the registered brand owners through Brand Registry"; an Amazon Ads account "in good standing with no policy violations"; and products in at least one of the listed categories (39 entries as the page lays them out, Health & Personal Care among them); "We don't support adult, used, or refurbished products". [official] (Sponsored Brands eligibility requirements, https://advertising.amazon.com/help/G5DAD7ZM3N639QF4, lastModified 2026-08-27). The SB product page widens this: "available for vendors, book vendors, KDP authors, agencies, and professional sellers enrolled in Amazon Brand Registry". [official] (Sponsored Brands | Amazon Ads, above)
- RULE. Authors and SB: the SB eligibility page's "at least 3 unique book titles under a single pen name" sits under its heading "Requirements to create ads for non-KDP books (including Audiobooks)"; the API release note of 2025-07-09 reads "Sponsored Brands is now available to all authors, including those with a single title", and the SB product page carries the news item "Sponsored Brands is now available for single-book authors". Read together, the three-title rule is stated for non-KDP books; the pages do not say so in one sentence (see Open questions). [official] (Sponsored Brands eligibility requirements, above; Release notes index, above; Sponsored Brands | Amazon Ads, above)
- RULE. Streaming TV eligibility: a Professional seller with Brand Registry, or a vendor including book vendors; "not for Health and Personal Care advertisers"; book vendors in the US only with three titles; books advertised only when offered by Amazon. [official] (Streaming TV eligibility requirements, https://advertising.amazon.com/help/GC2CYZXA7CU2NVPG)
- RULE. Stores: "Professional seller with Brand Registry and selling roles, or vendor" [official] (Stores eligibility requirements, https://advertising.amazon.com/help/G3Y9UFTFSJYPGY6X, lastModified 2026-05-13); Stores are free and open to "sellers enrolled in Brand Registry, vendors, and agencies" [official] (Stores, https://advertising.amazon.com/help/GEQXGAH9CZWU2K76).
- RULE. Display for non-Amazon sellers (brands that do not sell on Amazon) is US only, through the Partner Network, with a terms token and an ads account; location targeting is available only to non-Amazon sellers. The guide reads "At the moment, the US marketplace is supported for businesses that do not sell on Amazon", and "Sponsored Display locations are only available for advertisers that do not sell on Amazon". [official] (Get started with Sponsored Display for all businesses, https://advertising.amazon.com/API/docs/en-us/guides/sponsored-display/non-amazon-sellers/get-started; Sponsored Display locations, .../non-amazon-sellers/locations). The legacy `POST /adsAccounts` call it uses was deprecated in July 2026 with a July 2027 shutoff; its replacements are `POST /adsApi/v1/create/advertiserAccounts` and `POST /adsApi/v1/query/advertiserAccounts`, and since July 2026 "new client integrations are blocked". [official] (Deprecations, https://advertising.amazon.com/API/docs/en-us/release-notes/deprecations)
- RULE. Some ad products and features require business verification. "Most advertisers are automatically verified during registration"; otherwise the Verification Center lists tasks, and "All submissions are reviewed within 48 hours", with statuses Action Required, Available, Pending, Verified, Failed. [official] (Complete your business verification task, https://advertising.amazon.com/help/GJAXXZGBJWUJQZDW, lastModified 2026-09-01). The program-eligibility API has matching reasons `BUSINESS_NOT_VERIFIED` and `DVA_BUSINESS_VERIFICATION_NOT_COMPLETE` (§E).
- RULE. Amazon Business exclusive campaigns (`siteRestrictions: AMAZON_BUSINESS`) launched in the API for SP on 2025-04-24 and for SB on 2025-06-12, in US, CA, MX, DE, IT, FR, ES, UK, IN and JP; "these campaigns will only be shown on the Amazon Business store" and their reports reflect Amazon Business performance only. [official] (Release notes index, above)
- RULE. SB feature availability is uneven by marketplace: product targeting and negative product targeting in US, CA, DE, ES, FR, IT, UK, IN, JP; theme targeting in a wider list; moderation and pre-moderation in all marketplaces. [official] (Sponsored Brands features, https://advertising.amazon.com/API/docs/en-us/guides/sponsored-brands/features)

### C. Brand Registry

- RULE. Brand Registry is required for SB and for Stores, and the brand must have "an active registered trademark". [official] (Amazon Brand Registry for advertising, https://advertising.amazon.com/help/GKXEAMWZVR99QV37, lastModified 2025-05-26)
- RULE. "You must enroll in the Amazon Brand registry to use these features" (brand profile, SB, Brand Stores). To register, the owner provides "Your brand name. The brand name must have an active registered trademark", "The associated government-registered trademark number", and the product categories. Brand profiles also pass a moderation review against the brand profile content guidelines. [official] (Understand brands on Amazon Ads, https://advertising.amazon.com/help/GYD2WUGJKAMM6UNU)
- RULE. In the API, `GET /brands` "Gets an array of Brand data objects for the Brand associated with the profile"; each carries `brandId`, `brandEntityId` ("available exclusively for seller advertisers") and `brandRegistryName`. [official] (Sponsored Brands 3.0 contract, https://d3a0d0y2hgofx6.cloudfront.net/openapi/en-us/sponsored-brands/3-0/openapi.yaml)
- RULE. When a seller has no brand relation, the program-eligibility API returns `NO_BRAND_RELATIONS` — "Advertiser has no known relations to a brand." — at level `INELIGIBLE_WITH_RESOLUTION`; `NOT_BRAND_REPRESENTATIVE` is a separate reason. [official] (Eligibility contract, https://d1y2lf8k3vrkfu.cloudfront.net/openapi/en-us/dest/Eligibility_prod_3p.json)
- RULE. Pre-moderation rejects an SB brand name that does not match: `SPONSOR_NAME_MISMATCH` — "Brand name must match your registered brand name, or the name of a brand that you own or can legitimately represent." [official] (Pre-moderation guide, https://advertising.amazon.com/API/docs/en-us/guides/moderation/pre-moderation)
- RULE. Only own or authorised products may be advertised: the SP eligibility page requires that the advertiser advertise only products it owns or is authorised to sell. [official] (Sponsored Products eligibility requirements, above)

### D. Product eligibility: categories, condition, adult, Featured Offer, variations

- RULE. On the SP product page: "adult products, used products, refurbished products, and products in closed categories are not eligible". [official] (Sponsored Products | Amazon Ads, above)
- RULE. The SP eligibility page adds: a list of "Restricted products" to check (alcohol, art and industrial supplies, beauty, home-test kits, infant and baby, knives, OTC pharmaceuticals and healthcare, supplements and medical devices, religious products, sexual wellness, sports and outdoor products); suppressed listings cannot be advertised; virtual bundles are incompatible with SP; only child variations are eligible (not the parent); ad content must be in the marketplace's primary language. [official] (Sponsored Products eligibility requirements, https://advertising.amazon.com/help/GMJYF5GN8RNYB6HD)
- RULE. Book rules on the same page: a book in a language other than the marketplace's primary language carries a language disclaimer such as "(French Edition)"; author ads show only when Amazon is the Featured Offer; audiobooks can be advertised by vendors in AU, CA, FR, DE, IT, JP, ES, UK and US and by authors in the US, and sellers cannot advertise audiobooks; Author Central claims take up to 48 hours. [official] (Sponsored Products eligibility requirements, above)
- RULE. The Featured Offer gate, verbatim: "Sponsored Products and Display campaigns generate 0 impressions when the advertised ASIN or SKU doesn't hold the Featured Offer, even when the campaign shows "Delivering" status in Amazon Ads Agent." And: "Sponsored Brands campaigns, including Sponsored Brands video, aren't affected and continue to serve even when another seller holds the Featured Offer, as long as the advertised product is available to buy on Amazon." The gate is SKU-specific; a new campaign can take up to 72 hours to show impressions; resync takes 24–72 hours; adding the winning SKU resumes delivery within 24–48 hours. One factor listed for Featured Offer eligibility is an order defect rate below 1%, and "Even if you're the only seller for a product, Amazon may not assign the Featured Offer if the product fails low-price standards, has insufficient inventory, or lacks customer service infrastructure." [official] (Understand Featured Offer eligibility, https://advertising.amazon.com/help/GYVDGT5WNNW23LJ5, lastModified 2026-10-16). `areas/launch.md` §B reads the seller-level Featured Offer eligibility change of September 2026 from the Seller Central side; this file does not repeat it.
- RULE. The per-product eligibility API (Eligibility 3.0) is `POST /eligibility/product/list`. Body: `adType` (`sp` default, `sb`, `sd`, `dsp`), `productDetailsList` (at most 50 items, each `asin`, `sku`, optional `globalStoreSetting.catalogSourceCountryCode`), `locale`. Each `productResponseList[]` item carries `overallStatus` — `ELIGIBLE`, `ELIGIBLE_WITH_WARNING` or `INELIGIBLE`, which "Inherits highest severity" — and `eligibilityStatusList[]{name, message, helpUrl, severity}`. The `name` values are `ADULT_PRODUCT`, `CLOSED_CATEGORY`, `INELIGIBLE_CONDITION`, `INELIGIBLE_OFFER`, `INELIGIBLE_PRODUCT_COST`, `LISTING_SUPRESSED` (spelled so in the contract), `MISSING_IMAGE`, `MISSING_TITLE`, `NOT_IN_BUYBOX`, `OUT_OF_STOCK`, `RESTRICTED_CATEGORY`, `VARIATION_PARENT`. "Requests are permitted only for products sold by the merchant associated with the profile", and one ASIN may return several SKUs. [official] (Eligibility contract, https://d1y2lf8k3vrkfu.cloudfront.net/openapi/en-us/dest/Eligibility_prod_3p.json)
- RULE. Amazon's agent documentation describes the same call as a "Product Eligibility Skill" that "lets agents check per-ASIN advertising eligibility for a given ad type (Sponsored Products, Sponsored Brands, Sponsored Display, or DSP)" and "surfaces reason codes for ineligible products such as out-of-stock, restricted category, or policy violations"; the tool is named `eligibility-product_list`. [official] (Product Eligibility Skill, https://advertising.amazon.com/API/docs/en-us/mcp/skills/skills-library/product-eligibility)
- RULE. After launch, delivery is reported per entity in v1 as `DeliveryStatus` (`DELIVERING`, `LIMITED`, `NOT_DELIVERING`, `UNAVAILABLE`) with `DeliveryReason` values that include, among many, `NOT_IN_BUYBOX`, `NOT_BUYABLE`, `NO_PURCHASABLE_OFFER`, `OUT_OF_STOCK`, `NO_INVENTORY`, `LANDING_PAGE_INELIGIBLE`, `BRAND_INELIGIBLE`, `ADVERTISER_INELIGIBLE`, `NOT_IN_POLICY`, `TARGET_BLOCKED`, `CREATIVE_REJECTED`, `CREATIVE_PENDING_REVIEW`, `ADVERTISER_POLICING_PENDING_REVIEW`, `ADVERTISER_POLICING_SUSPENDED`, the `MODERATION_*` family (for example `MODERATION_ADULT_PRODUCT_POLICY_VIOLATION`, `MODERATION_CLAIM_WEIGHTLOSS_POLICY_VIOLATION`, `MODERATION_HFSS_PRODUCT_POLICY_VIOLATION`, `MODERATION_ERROR_404`, `MODERATION_NOT_COMPLIANT_TO_AD_POLICY`), `SPEND_LIMIT_EXCEEDED`, `ADVERTISER_PAYMENT_FAILURE` and `ADVERTISER_OUT_OF_PREPAY_BALANCE`. Of the 93 `DeliveryReason` values on 2026-11-09, 85 carry no description in the contract. [official] (v1 merged contract, above). The older "Computed status and campaign status" page marks its own `NOT_IN_BUYBOX` ad status "This field is no longer supported", while the v1 `DeliveryReason` enum and the per-product eligibility enum still carry `NOT_IN_BUYBOX`. [official] (Computed status, https://advertising.amazon.com/API/docs/en-us/reference/concepts/computed-status)
- GUIDANCE. "Choose which products to advertise" advises starting "with one or just a handful of well-performing products" that share a category, keywords or price points, "have at least a four-star rating", have detail pages "enriched with high-quality or A+ brand content", and are in stock — the four-star figure for human readers only. ### F. Sponsored Products: placements, video, off-Amazon, books

- RULE. SP serves on Amazon, Amazon Business and, in the US, on premium publishers (Pinterest, Buzzfeed and Hearst are named); "Amazon and beyond" is the default reach setting. [official] (Understand Sponsored Products, https://advertising.amazon.com/help/GJUCNANNV3GQVXJZ, lastModified 2026-10-20)
- RULE. Off-Amazon SP runs in 19 countries (BE, BR, CA, EG, FR, DE, IN, IE, IT, MX, NL, PL, SA, ES, SE, TR, AE, UK, US on the page). The setting is "Increase reach off-Amazon" (default) or "Limit reach to Amazon". The max bid applies off-Amazon; placement adjustments do not, but dynamic and rule-based bidding do. Placement reports split "Off Amazon" and "Other – On Amazon", and deny lists apply. [official] (Understand Sponsored Products off-Amazon advertising, https://advertising.amazon.com/help/GYTD2Z3SYMAAMVXA, lastModified 2026-10-31). [official] (v1 merged contract, above; Release notes index, above)
- RULE. Off-Amazon SP reaches "premium sites, apps, and in creator content" by three routes: third-party sites and apps; third-party sites and apps "with Amazon investment", where Amazon "invests its own marketing funds alongside your campaign"; and "Creator content: Creators in the Amazon Influencer and Associates Program with a track record of driving traffic or sales on Amazon can select your campaign to feature in their product reviews, editorial content, and buying guides." For creator content "Amazon calculates your CPC based on your maximum bid, product's expected performance and sales the ad generates. Creators may receive a portion of the CPC from qualifying clicks. You're only charged when a shopper clicks on a creator's recommendation and is taken to a detail page or adds to cart." "Limit reach to Amazon" keeps ads on "Amazon owned-and-operated properties, including creator storefronts". In the Placement Report creator content off Amazon shows as "Off Amazon" and a creator's storefront on Amazon as "Other – On Amazon". [official] (Understand Sponsored Products off-Amazon advertising, above, lastModified 2026-10-31). The creator-placement start date of 2026-08-10 is not on this page or in the API release notes read; it comes from an advertiser notice reported by PPC Land and Ad Badger (What practitioners add). Creators are excluded through the account deny list, which takes "creator tags" (the `tag=` value in the creator's promotional URL) beside web domains and app IDs, up to 10,000 lines per file, and "These deny lists are applied to all active Sponsored Products and Display campaigns". [official] (Manage deny lists, https://advertising.amazon.com/help/GFDAZEU76AZZ8K3A, lastModified 2026-10-09)
- RULE. SP video "is only available in the US and is currently not available for Books". Two routes: manual upload, and auto-assembled video, "an opt-in feature where we create a video ad from video assets you've already uploaded or that appear on your product detail page. No new video content is generated." Billing is CPC on the existing budget and bid, with an optional video bid adjustment that applies "on top of other placement adjustments". [official] (Understand Sponsored Products video, https://advertising.amazon.com/help/G45ASMDJY8XXQQFK, lastModified 2026-10-20)
- RULE. The SP video guidelines require the product to fill at least half the frame and the video to carry no audio. Amazon's pages disagree on minimum length (the help pages say "at least 6 seconds", the API ad-extension guide says "Minimum duration | 7 seconds"), and the guidelines page itself gives two feature-title limits ("up to 3 words, 15 characters or less" in one place, "a total of 22 characters or less" in another). [official] (Sponsored Products video guidelines, https://advertising.amazon.com/help/GGXB3X3PWHQBRY2M; Ad extensions overview, https://advertising.amazon.com/API/docs/en-us/guides/sponsored-products/ad-extensions/overview)
- RULE. In the API, video extensions are US only, need the account to be enabled, accept MP4/MOV, 16:9, up to 500 MB, 1–5 clips, with an `optimizeText` option, and are written through `POST /adsApi/v1/update/ads` and read through `query/adExtensions`. [official] (Ad extensions overview, above; Auto-assembled videos, https://advertising.amazon.com/API/docs/en-us/guides/sponsored-products/auto-assembled-videos)

### R. The moderation APIs

- RULE. Pre-moderation, `POST /preModeration`, checks components before a campaign is submitted. `adProgram` is one of the DSP values, `SPONSORED_BRANDS`, `SPONSORED_BRANDS_SPOTLIGHT`, `SPONSORED_BRANDS_VIDEO`, `SPONSORED_DISPLAY`, `SPONSORED_DISPLAY_NOT_SOLD_ON_AMAZON`, `SPONSORED_TV` or `STORES`. Components (up to 10 per type, video at most 1): asin (`LANDING_ASIN`, `PRODUCT_ASIN`), date (`CAMPAIGN_DATE`, `CAMPAIGN_DATES`), third-party (`ADSP_3P_DISPLAY`, `ADSP_3P_VIDEO`), image (`BRAND_LOGO`, `CUSTOM_IMAGE`, `CUSTOM_IMAGE_RESPONSIVE_SIZE`, `CUSTOM_IMAGE_SIZE_SPECIFIC`, `OTHER_IMAGE`, by public URL), text (`BRAND_NAME`, `HEADLINE`, `OTHER_TEXT`, with `corrections[]` in the response), video (`SPONSORED_BRANDS_VIDEO`, `SPONSORED_DISPLAY_VIDEO`, `SPONSORED_TV_VIDEO`, `DSP_VIDEO`, `OTHER_VIDEO`) and url (`CLICK_THROUGH_URL`). Violation type is `REJECTED` or `WARNING`; status `APPROVED`, `FAILED`, `REJECTED` or `RETRYABLE_FAILURE`. "We recommend to send all components of the same entity to be sent together ... In one request please don't send the components of more than one entity." [official] (Pre-moderation contract and guide, above)
- RULE. The pre-moderation guide publishes the rejection codes and messages per program: for SB, among others, `PUNCTUATIONS`, `ELLIPSIS`, `GRAMMAR`, `LISTING_PRICES` ("Numeric pricing, deals, or savings such as '$4.99' or 'Save 50%' are not allowed"), `CAP_WORDS` ("All CAPS, CaMeL case, and random capitalization are not allowed"), `SPECIAL_CHARACTERS`, `AMAZON_SPECIFIC_TERM`, `SPONSOR_NAME_MISMATCH`, `UNSUBSTANTIATED_CLAIM_VIOLATION`, `CUSTOM_IMAGE_TEXT`, `INVALID_CUSTOM_IMAGE`, `INVALID_BRAND_LOGO`, `LANGUAGE_UNSUPPORTED` and the `FATAL_*` codes for adult, profane, critical-events (political) and drug content; for SB video also `PILLAR_BOX`, `LETTER_BOX` and `WINDOW_FRAME`; for Display `TIME_SENSITIVE_EVENT` ("Time-sensitive promotions are prohibited unless communicated dynamically"); and Stores and DSP lists. The contract's image spec-violation enum has 12 values, among them `CUSTOM_IMAGE_CONTAINS_TEXT`, `LANGUAGE_MISMATCH`, `CTA_IN_ALL_CAPS` and `POOR_IMAGE_OR_VIDEO_QUALITY` (the guide shows these in its DSP image table), and the video spec-violation enum has 22 values. [official] (Pre-moderation guide and contract, above)
- RULE. Moderation results, `POST /moderation/results` (media type `application/vnd.moderationresultsrequest.v4.1+json`, permission `advertiser_campaign_view`), "supports only SponsoredBrands, SponsoredProducts and SponsoredDisplay". Request: `adProgramType` (`SB_PRODUCT_COLLECTION`, `SB_STORE_SPOTLIGHT`, `SB_VIDEO`, `SPONSORED_DISPLAY`, `SPONSORED_PRODUCTS`), `id` with `idType: AD_ID`, `maxResults` 1–10, `moderationStatusFilter`, `versionIdFilter`, `nextToken`. Response `moderationResults[]`: `moderationStatus` (`APPROVED`, `FAILED`, `IN_PROGRESS`, `REJECTED`); `etaForModeration` (ISO 8601 UTC, only while `IN_PROGRESS`); `policyViolations[]` with `policyDescription`, `policyLinkUrl`, and the evidence — `violatingTextContents` (the reviewed text and the violating span with start and end positions), `violatingImageContents` (a crop), `violatingVideoContents` (start and end times) and `violatingAsinContents`; `componentModerationResults` for SP image, text and video components; `versionId` (the SB creative version). [official] (Moderation contract, above)
- RULE. In v1, SB and STV creative settings carry `CreativeStatus.moderationStatus`: `APPROVED_WITH_EXCEPTIONS`, `PENDING_TRANSLATION`, `PUBLISHED`, `REJECTED_BY_MODERATION`, `SUBMITTED_FOR_MODERATION`. [official] (v1 merged contract, above)

### V. Creators: Creator Connections, the Influencer and Associates Programs, and SP creator placements

Three things share the word "creator" and are paid three different ways: Creator Connections (a brand pays a bonus commission on sales), the Amazon Influencer and Associates Programs (Amazon pays creators its own affiliate commission, and a share of CPC on sponsored clicks), and Sponsored Products creator placements (the advertiser pays CPC from the SP campaign, §F).

- RULE. What Creator Connections is. "Creator Connections is Amazon's marketplace service that connects brands on Amazon with Amazon Creators. This service allows brands to offer bonus commissions in exchange for qualifying sales generated by content created by Amazon Creators." The brand selects "eligible products from your catalog, set the commission rate, specify the duration (start and end date), and allocate a maximum budget for your Affiliate+ campaign"; creators "opt into your campaign"; it is "Pay-for-performance ... only paying for qualifying sales", "without additional service fees". Creators are publishers, influencers, and deal sites and bloggers. [official] (Understand Amazon Creator Connections, https://advertising.amazon.com/help/GPARAL6JVGFEJKG2, lastModified 2026-10-26)
- RULE. Brand eligibility: "Advertise in the United States through an Amazon Ads account based in the United States, China, or Hong Kong"; "Register in Brand Registry as the brand representative or authorized reseller"; "Accept the Terms & Conditions". Sellers reach it in Seller Central under Advertising > Creator Connections; vendors in the ads console "with your Brands Store account" under Brand Content; admins grant secondary users access under Settings > User Permissions; "New advertisers meeting all eligibility requirements may need to allow up to 7-10 business days to gain access." Creator eligibility: a publisher, influencer or blogger "with a qualifying online presence", in "the Amazon Influencer and Associates Program", at "Bronze tier or higher in Creator Stars, a tiered benefits program that considers Creator sales and engagement." [official] (Understand Amazon Creator Connections, above). Amazon's older brand guide (PDF, created 2025-03-04, cover marked "Amazon Confidential" but served publicly) required "the brand representative" only; the 2026 help pages add "or authorized reseller". [official] (Creator Connections Brand Guide, https://m.media-amazon.com/images/G/28/TWGS/SU/AmazonCreatorConnections_BrandGuide.pdf)
- RULE. Access history, from Amazon staff in the Seller Forums: earlier, "The creator Connections access for Sellers is currently closed" (Roberto_Amazon, a reply the page shows as "2 years ago", in a thread its metadata dates 2024-05-10); in a thread dated 2024-12-06 "We are dialing up Creator Connections access to all eligible advertisers and you do not need to submit an access request", with eligibility checked by "the billing settings of your Amazon Ad Console account are US, CN, or HK (Administration > Sponsored Ads Billing)", and "We expect all eligible advertisers to have access by the end of 2024." (Danny_Amazon) [official, Amazon staff forum replies] (Creator Connections Access (Seller Central), https://sellercentral.amazon.com/seller-forums/discussions/t/bb21a715-56e0-4f8a-843e-b6fb5a4ef58e; Creator Connections - how to apply, https://sellercentral.amazon.com/seller-forums/discussions/t/6d95f804-2357-4cca-a84c-c0b935eca58f; both read 2026-11-09)
- RULE. Campaign terms (for human readers only, the live console holds the figures): name up to 80 characters; message to creators up to 5,000 characters, with required disclosures ("Sponsored," "Advertisement," or "Ad"); 30–365 days; "Campaign budget: Enter the maximum amount (minimum $5,000) to spend on Creator commissions"; "Commission rate: Set the percentage (minimum 10%)"; "up to 1,200 ASINs per campaign"; 24 hours to edit or cancel, after which "you can only extend the end date or increase the budget" (the same page also says the creator message can be modified once live); creators cannot be chosen ("the campaign will be visible to all eligible Creators"); messages only to creators who accepted; no samples through the program. [official] (Create and manage a Creator Connections campaign, https://advertising.amazon.com/help/GMANECFAFD49W23Y, lastModified 2026-05-12). The brand guide adds: up to 800 creators per campaign, first come first served; creators cannot be removed or rejected once joined; a creator who accepts two campaigns on one ASIN earns from one; the creator owns the content, which the brand may repost "but you cannot use it in any paid marketing"; "We do not offer any indemnity against liability"; an exhausted budget ends the campaign early. [official] (Creator Connections Brand Guide, above)
- RULE. How it is charged: "Your Affiliate+ commission is calculated on the net price the customer paid, after any coupons or discounts are applied", for direct sales (the linked product or a featured size/colour variation) and indirect sales (another product of the brand "that's still part of the campaign"). "There are no base fees or daily fees. You're only charged when a customer orders your SKU. If another seller takes the featured offer and a customer orders that seller's SKU instead, that order is not attributed to your Creator Connections campaign and no commission is charged." Unspent budget, including the $5,000 minimum, is not invoiced. Creators keep their standard Associates or Influencer commission on top of the bonus. [official] (Understand Amazon Creator Connections, above; Creator Connections Brand Guide, above)
- RULE. How its sales are reported: "Sales are attributed to your Affiliate+ campaign when a customer purchases within 24 hours of clicking a Creator's link. This window closes once the customer completes their purchase, or the customer clicks through a different Creator's link." Reports are "available at the campaign level and include metrics for sales, orders, and clicks" in "the Creator Connections reporting portal", downloadable; "Creator Connections orders are not linked to the order management center ... Order-level tracking is not available; reports show aggregate data only." Sales "are finalized within 7 days after the campaign ends, based on whether the products were shipped", excluding orders "canceled within 72 hours or fail payment". Charges appear "on your Amazon Advertising invoice under the campaign title along with "Sponsored Ads" as a separate line item, billed every 30 days." [official] (Understand Amazon Creator Connections; Create and manage a Creator Connections campaign, above). In the Ads API the program appears only on the invoice: the billing contract's `invoiceLine` carries `programName`, whose `adProgram` enum ("Sponsored Ads only") is `AMAZON LIVE`, `CREATOR CONNECTIONS`, `SPONSORED BRANDS`, `SPONSORED DISPLAY`, `SPONSORED DISPLAY FOR FIRE TV`, `SPONSORED PRODUCT`, beside `campaignId`, `campaignName`, `commissionAmount`, `commissionRate`, `cost`, `costEventType` (`CLICKS`, `IMPRESSIONS`), `fees`, `promotionAmount` and `supplyCost`; `invoiceLines` is "For Sponsored Ads ... a per-campaign breakdown of charges". [official] (Advertising Billing contract, https://dtrnk0o2zy01c.cloudfront.net/openapi/en-us/dest/AdvertisingBilling_prod_3p.json). No endpoint, report type or v1 entity for Creator Connections' sales, orders or clicks appears in the contracts index, the v1 merged contract or the release notes read on 2026-11-09 [seat, from those reads].
- Reported, not RULE. Sponsored Content Requests is a separate, invite-only part of Creator Connections reported by trade press and agencies: flat-fee creator content with rounds of revisions, licensed for reuse in Sponsored Brands ads for up to 90 days (Modern Retail, 2025-10-16; Paragon, undated). No Amazon page on it was found by GET or search on 2026-11-09. [opinion] (see What practitioners add)
- RULE. The Amazon Influencer and Associates Programs as a surface: "Content creators participating in the Amazon Influencer and Associate Programs earn affiliate commissions on purchases and ad revenue from clicks on sponsored products." Qualifying needs "a YouTube, Instagram, Tiktok, Facebook or Pinterest account"; creator content on Amazon is labelled with the program name; a creator's profile links to "their Influencer or Associate Storefront"; "Influencers and Associates may choose to recommend sponsored products that will allow them to earn ad revenue from clicks", and "Influencers may also choose to partner with brands to earn bonus commissions for sponsored content." [official] (Amazon Influencer and Associate Programs, https://www.amazon.com/shop/info). Creator storefronts are Amazon owned-and-operated supply in the SP off-Amazon setting (§F). Amazon Ads' guide to social strategies counts "more than one million Amazon creators" and names livestreams, idea lists and shoppable videos as creator formats, and audiences of "Amazon influencer storefronts" visitors for DVA retargeting. [official] (Scale social strategies with Amazon Ads, https://advertising.amazon.com/library/guides/scale-social-strategies-with-amazon-ads, datePublished 2025-06-05)
- RULE. Where the two creator routes meet. SP creator placements are bought by the SP campaign (CPC, campaign budget, the off-Amazon setting, creator tags on the deny list) and reported in the SP Placement Report as "Off Amazon" or "Other – On Amazon" (§F); Creator Connections is bought by commission on sales, outside any SP campaign, and reported only in its own portal. In India the two names cross: Amazon Ads' Connected Worlds India recap (2026-06-29) says "Creator Connections, launching in India later this year, will automatically connect Sponsored Product campaigns with creators from the Amazon Influencer Program", and names an "Amazon Ads Creator Program" of "over 500,000 creators". [official] (Amazon Ads Connected Worlds India event recap, https://advertising.amazon.com/library/news/connected-worlds-india-flagship-event, datePublished 2026-06-29). So in India "Creator Connections" is used for what the US help pages describe as SP creator content [seat].

## Published research

- [research] Dash, Ghosh, Mukherjee, Chakraborty and Gummadi, "Sponsored is the New Organic: Implications of Sponsored Results on Quality of Search Results in the Amazon Marketplace" (arXiv:2407.19099, 2024-07-26; AIES 2024). They simulated 4,800 searches in four Amazon country stores (about 2M organic and 638K sponsored results) and found that "items with poor organic ranks (beyond 100th position) appear as sponsored results even before the top organic results on the first page", and that "in majority of the cases, these top sponsored results are costlier and are of poorer quality than the top organic results". This bears on eligibility only indirectly: Amazon's gates (Featured Offer, category, condition) admit products to the auction regardless of organic rank. (https://arxiv.org/abs/2407.19099)

## What cx must read at run time

All Ads API calls go through the open door, `cx raw amazon-ads --origin <regional origin> --method <M> --path <path>`, with the profile scope header as `method/ads-api-surface.md` describes. Figures recorded in this file are for human readers; the live values come from these reads.

Who the advertiser is and where it may advertise:

- `GET /v2/profiles` (optionally `profileTypeFilter`, `validPaymentMethodFilter`): per profile `countryCode`, `currencyCode`, `accountInfo.type` (`seller`, `vendor`, `agency`), `accountInfo.subType` (`KDP_AUTHOR`, `AMAZON_ATTRIBUTION`), `validPaymentMethod` ("Only present for Vendors"). At most 5,000 profiles per response, and only those of the host's region. (Profiles contract; Profiles guide)
- `POST /adsApi/v1/query/advertiserAccounts` where the caller works with Advertiser Accounts (the legacy `/adsAccounts` calls shut off in July 2027). (Deprecations)
- `POST /eligibility/programs` with `Amazon-Ads-AccountId`, media type `application/vnd.programeligibility.v2+json`, paging by `nextToken`: per `marketplaceId`, per `adProgram` (`SB`, `SD`, `SPOT`, `DTC`, `MAAS`), `eligible` and `reasons[]{code, description, level}`. The reason enum is read from the response, not from this file's copy; the contract's v2 example uses an `eligibilityStatusMaps` shape that differs from its schema, so a reader that accepts both shapes reads either. (Eligibility contract)
- `GET /brands` (SB 3.0): `brandId`, `brandEntityId`, `brandRegistryName` — the brands the profile can advertise in SB and Stores. (Sponsored Brands 3.0 contract)
- `POST /brand/stores/v1/stores/list` (media type `application/vnd.bhstoreslist.v1+json` or `application/brandStores.ListStores.v1+json`): `stores[]{brandEntityId, storeId, storeName, storeStatus}`, paged by `maxResults` (1–30) and `nextToken`; then `POST /brand/stores/v1/storePages/list` with `identifier` (brand entity id or store id) and `identifierType` for `storePages[]{storePageId, storePageName, storePageStatus, storePageUrl}`. These replace `GET /v2/stores`, shut off on June 1, 2026, which the Brand Store overview guide still shows. (Brand Home 3.0 contract; Deprecations)

Whether a product may be advertised:

- `POST /eligibility/product/list` with `adType` `sp`, `sb` or `sd`, up to 50 `{asin, sku}` per call, `locale`: `overallStatus` and `eligibilityStatusList[]{name, message, helpUrl, severity}`. (Eligibility contract)
- For SP Featured Offer state and listing state, the SP-API reads named in `areas/launch.md` (§B–C) apply: Product Pricing's Featured Offer reads for the SKU and Listings Items issues for suppression. This seat did not re-open the SP-API models for this file [seat]. Keepa's Buy Box history fields (`buyBoxSellerIdHistory` and the Buy Box statistics in `stats`) give the history where SP-API gives only the present; `areas/diagnosis.md` names them for the delivery question [seat].
- After launch, v1 delivery state: `deliveryStatus` and `deliveryReasons[]` on campaigns, ad groups and ads (`POST /adsApi/v1/query/campaigns`, `.../adGroups`, `.../ads`). (v1 merged contract; Computed status)
- For SB store and brand-video landing pages: `GET /pageAsins?pageUrl=<Store page>` — only ASINs placed directly on that page. (Sponsored Brands 3.0 contract)

Whether creative will pass, and why it did not:

- `POST /preModeration` with `adProgram`, `locale`, `targetLanguage` and the components of one entity: `policyViolations` by component with `violationType` `REJECTED` or `WARNING`, and `corrections[]` for text. (Pre-moderation contract)
- `POST /moderation/results` (media type `application/vnd.moderationresultsrequest.v4.1+json`) with `adProgramType`, `id`, `idType: AD_ID`, `maxResults` ≤ 10: `moderationStatus`, `etaForModeration`, `policyViolations[]{policyDescription, policyLinkUrl, violating*Contents}`, `componentModerationResults`, `versionId`. (Moderation contract)
- v1 creative status for SB and STV ads: `creativeStatus.moderationStatus`. (v1 merged contract)
- STV: `POST /st/creatives/moderations/list` and `POST /st/creatives/moderations/policyViolations/list`. (Sponsored TV contract)
- SD: creative `moderationStatus` on `GET /sd/creatives` (the SD 3.0 creatives resource). (Sponsored Display creatives; SD 3.0 contract)
- The policy page a violation names is the `policyLinkUrl` itself; a skill reads that link rather than a stored copy of the policy.

AI shopping surfaces:

- Prompt extensions per ad: `POST /adsApi/v1/query/adExtensions` (`adExtensionType: PROMPTS`, `promptText`, status). (v1 merged contract)
- Prompt performance: report types `spPromptAdExtension`, `sbPromptAdExtension` (`groupBy: promptAdExtension`, US, 90-day range) through v3 `POST /reporting/reports`, deprecated with data delivery ending June 30, 2027; the replacement is the reporting API v1. (Prompt ad extension report; Deprecations)
- SP video extensions: report `spVideoAdExtension` (`groupBy: videoAdExtension`, US, 90-day range, also v3) per the video-ad-extension report guide, and `query/adExtensions` with `adExtensionType: VIDEO`; auto-assembled opt-out state is the `optOuts` field on `POST /adsApi/v1/query/campaigns` (documented in the auto-assembled videos guide, absent from the merged contract on 2026-11-09). (Video ad extension report, https://advertising.amazon.com/API/docs/en-us/guides/reporting/v3/report-types/video-ad-extension; v1 merged contract)

- Alexa for Shopping and Alexa+: no API read names either surface. What exists is the prompt extension reads above, the campaign totals they fall into, and the v1 ad-group `inventoryType` value `ALEXA_DEVICES` on the DVA side. Whether a marketplace has Alexa for Shopping or Rufus is read from Amazon's public pages at run time, not from the API. (v1 merged contract; What agentic shopping means for advertising)

Creators:

- SP creator placements: the campaign's off-Amazon setting (`offAmazonBudgetControlStrategy`, §F, owned by `areas/bids-and-placements.md`) and the placement split ("Off Amazon", "Other – On Amazon") in SP placement reporting; the account deny list (web, app and creator tags) has no API read in the contracts read, and is a console page. (Understand Sponsored Products off-Amazon advertising; Manage deny lists)
- Creator Connections: no Ads API read. Its spend, sales, orders and clicks live in the Creator Connections portal (Seller Central > Advertising for sellers, ads console > Brand Content for vendors), and its charges in the advertising invoice as a separate line titled with the campaign name. `GET /invoices/{invoiceId}`: `invoiceLines[]` with `programName` `CREATOR CONNECTIONS`, `campaignName`, `commissionAmount`, `commissionRate` (the `nemo_transactions_view` or `_edit` permission, §T). That is the Creator Connections spend; its sales are not in the API. Sales the program attributes are also ordinary orders in SP-API sales and traffic reads, with no flag that marks them as creator-driven [seat]. (Understand Amazon Creator Connections; Advertising Billing contract)

Money:

- `POST /billing/statuses` (`billingStatusCode`), `POST /billing/notifications` (`name`, `severity`, `impact`), `GET /invoices`, `GET /invoices/{invoiceId}`, `POST /invoiceSummaries/list`. Each needs a billing permission that the cx grant may or may not include; a 403 here is a permission answer, not an account state. (Advertising Billing contract)

## Positions

**P-ad-products-and-eligibility-1.** Sponsored Products is open to professional sellers, vendors, book vendors, KDP authors and agencies, and a product must be in an eligible category and eligible for the Featured Offer to be advertised. (Sponsored Products | Amazon Ads, https://advertising.amazon.com/solutions/products/sponsored-products) [official]

**P-ad-products-and-eligibility-2.** Sponsored Brands is available only to vendors and to professional sellers enrolled in Amazon Brand Registry, and Brand Registry requires a brand name with an active registered trademark. (Understand Sponsored Brands, https://advertising.amazon.com/help/GGWFYHL27MFXLHS6; Understand brands on Amazon Ads, https://advertising.amazon.com/help/GYD2WUGJKAMM6UNU) [official]

**P-ad-products-and-eligibility-3.** Display (Sponsored Display) requires a vendor account or a professional seller with Brand Registry, bills by CPC or vCPM, and serves on and off Amazon; its audiences tactic is open to sellers, vendors and non-Amazon sellers, and the non-Amazon-seller path is US only. Amazon's Sponsored Display product page says Sponsored Display "is now known as display ads" within Amazon Ads Agent, with existing campaigns reached under Display, Video, and Audio campaigns. (Understand Display, https://advertising.amazon.com/help/GHYXY6HJRDM5VCCY; Sponsored Display overview, https://advertising.amazon.com/API/docs/en-us/guides/sponsored-display/overview; Sponsored Display – Create display advertising campaigns, https://advertising.amazon.com/solutions/products/sponsored-display) [official]

**P-ad-products-and-eligibility-4.** Streaming TV requires a professional seller with Brand Registry or a vendor, is not available to Health and Personal Care advertisers, and is in open beta through the API in BR, CA, MX, UK and US. (Streaming TV eligibility requirements, https://advertising.amazon.com/help/GC2CYZXA7CU2NVPG; Sponsored TV overview, https://advertising.amazon.com/API/docs/en-us/guides/sponsored-tv/overview) [official]

**P-ad-products-and-eligibility-5.** A profile's `accountInfo.type` is `seller`, `vendor` or `agency`, with `KDP_AUTHOR` as a sub-type, and Amazon states that seller accounts need Brand Registry for SB and Display. (Profiles contract, https://d3a0d0y2hgofx6.cloudfront.net/openapi/en-us/profiles/3-0/openapi.yaml; Profiles guide) [official]

**P-ad-products-and-eligibility-6.** SP and Display campaigns generate zero impressions for an ASIN or SKU that does not hold the Featured Offer, even when the campaign shows "Delivering"; SB, including SB video, is not affected while the product is buyable. Rel.ai calls Featured Offer loss the most common cause of a sudden SP impression drop. (Understand Featured Offer eligibility, https://advertising.amazon.com/help/GYVDGT5WNNW23LJ5) [official]; (Rel.ai, https://rel.ai/blog/buy-box-impression-drops) [opinion]

**P-ad-products-and-eligibility-7.** Adult, used, refurbished and closed-category products are not eligible for SP; variation parents and suppressed listings cannot be advertised with SP, and only child variations are eligible. (Sponsored Products | Amazon Ads; Sponsored Products eligibility requirements, https://advertising.amazon.com/help/GMJYF5GN8RNYB6HD) [official]

**P-ad-products-and-eligibility-8.** SB does not support adult, used or refurbished products and is limited to a listed set of product categories. (Sponsored Brands eligibility requirements, https://advertising.amazon.com/help/G5DAD7ZM3N639QF4) [official]

**P-ad-products-and-eligibility-9.** `POST /eligibility/product/list` returns, per product and ad type, an overall status of `ELIGIBLE`, `ELIGIBLE_WITH_WARNING` or `INELIGIBLE` that inherits the highest severity among named reasons such as `NOT_IN_BUYBOX`, `OUT_OF_STOCK`, `VARIATION_PARENT`, `ADULT_PRODUCT` and `RESTRICTED_CATEGORY`, and accepts only products sold by the profile's merchant. (Eligibility contract, https://d1y2lf8k3vrkfu.cloudfront.net/openapi/en-us/dest/Eligibility_prod_3p.json) [official]

**P-ad-products-and-eligibility-10.** `POST /eligibility/programs` returns per marketplace and per ad program (`SB`, `SD`, `SPOT`, `DTC`, `MAAS`) whether the account is eligible, with reason codes whose level is `INELIGIBLE` or `INELIGIBLE_WITH_RESOLUTION`; `NO_BRAND_RELATIONS` means the advertiser has no known relation to a brand. (Eligibility contract) [official]

**P-ad-products-and-eligibility-11.** Program-eligibility reasons cover account standing, terms, brand relation, business verification, tax, billing and payment state, and feature access, among them `ADS_TERMS_NOT_ACCEPTED`, `BUSINESS_NOT_VERIFIED`, `TAX_INFO_NOT_COMPLETE`, `PAYMENT_METHOD_NOT_VALID` and `KDP_AUTHOR_NOT_ELIGIBLE`. (Eligibility contract) [official]

### Seat's positions (enter no skill)

- The ad policy is now spread over about twenty help-center pages whose ids redirect as Amazon reorganises; the `policyLinkUrl` in a moderation result is the stable pointer from a rejection to its rule. [seat]
