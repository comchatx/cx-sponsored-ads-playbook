# Vendor promotions

## Reports

Amazon's promotion performance report covers vendor promotions, with types including Best Deal, Deal of the Day, Lightning Deal, Price Discount, Sales Discount, Coupon and Promo Code. (E24)
Its statuses include `NEEDS_YOUR_ATTENTION`, and its creation channels are self-service, full-service (account managers), automated (for example Deal-O-Matic) and manual (Vendisto). (E24)
It carries the vendor-only funding agreement id and the funding amount spent, times in UTC, and two years of start dates. (E24)
The vendor coupon report gives clips, gross redemptions, total discount and the vendor's budget spent, including clip and redemption fees, per coupon and campaign, in UTC. (E24)
The SP-API Promotions API (2025-12-01) is available to sellers; vendor promotions are reported through the promotion performance and coupon reports. (E24)

## Amazon's 2017 vendor guides

Amazon's March 2017 vendor self-service promotions guide listed Price Discount up to 12 months, submitted at least 24 hours ahead. (E25)
It listed Best Deal up to 2 weeks, at least 10% off, submitted 4 or more weeks ahead. (E25)
It listed Lightning Deal up to 6 hours, at least 15% off, with a maximum unit quantity, submitted 4 or more weeks ahead. (E25)
It listed Promo Code up to 30 days, at least 15% off, submitted at least 24 hours ahead. (E25)
The guide recommended 4 weeks for all, "or Amazon may not have time to buy inventory", and said minimum discounts may rise for Prime Day, Black Friday and Cyber Monday. (E25)
It stated that Amazon often buys more product to prepare for an approved promotion, sets prices independently, generally aims to pass promotional funding on to customers, and may not pass it on if the product does not meet financial thresholds. (E25)
Amazon's August 2017 AMS toolkit stated that "Product Display Ads are the only ad unit that supports VPCs" (vendor-powered coupons) and that Subscribe & Save VPCs were not eligible. (E25)
The 2017 toolkit stated that a Product Display ad whose VPC is no longer valid reverts to the normal format. (E25)

## Funding

Amazon's purchase-order model carries a `dealCode`, a promotional or deal number used to obtain a price discount on items on the order. (E27)
AMALYTIX (Trutz Fries, October 2026) states that vendors fund Lightning Deal discounts per unit, in local currency and excluding tax, and names no separate vendor deal fee. (E27)
Wake Commerce (James Wakefield, 2024) describes co-op, also called ContraCoGS, as deducted through PO invoices. (E27)
