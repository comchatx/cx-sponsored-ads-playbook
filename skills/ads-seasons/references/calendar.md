# Event calendar

An event's dates, hours and countries differ by marketplace and year. (E1, E2)
Amazon's Sponsored Products events operation returns special events with suggested date ranges in the advertiser's marketplace. (E3)

## Prime Day

The Amazon Ads "Prime Day" events page (https://advertising.amazon.com/events/prime-day) gives Prime Day's dates and the countries it runs in, and aboutamazon's "When is Amazon Prime Day" (https://www.aboutamazon.com/news/retail/when-is-amazon-prime-day) its US hours. (E1)
The countries where Prime Day comes to Amazon Business are listed in Amazon Ads' "Prime Day 2026: Advanced advertising strategies" (https://advertising.amazon.com/library/guides/prime-day-guide-advanced-strategies). (E1)
Since 2019 Prime Day has started as early as June (2021) and as late as October (2020), as aboutamazon's "The history of Prime Day" records (https://www.aboutamazon.com/news/retail/the-history-of-prime-day). (E1)
Prime Day changed from 48 to 96 hours in 2025. (E1)

## Autumn and holiday

Prime Big Deal Days' US dates and hours are on aboutamazon's "Prime Big Deal Days FAQ" (https://www.aboutamazon.com/news/retail/amazon-prime-big-deal-days-faq). (E1)
Its local start time differs by marketplace, and Germany calls it "Prime Deal Days": About Amazon UK's Prime Big Deal Days page (https://www.aboutamazon.co.uk/news/retail/amazon-prime-big-deal-days-2026-date) gives the UK's start time, About Amazon EU's Prime Big Deal Days page (https://www.aboutamazon.eu/news/retail/prime-big-deal-days-2026) the European start time and countries, and About Amazon Deutschland's "Was sind die Amazon Prime Deal Days?" (https://www.aboutamazon.de/news/amazon-prime-und-shopping/prime-deal-days-termine-angebote) the German dates. (E2)
Amazon's German release says dates may vary by country. (E2)
The US Black Friday and Cyber Monday dates are on aboutamazon's "When is Black Friday 2026? Here's everything you need to know" (https://www.aboutamazon.com/news/retail/when-is-amazon-black-friday-faq) and "When is Cyber Monday 2026?" (https://www.aboutamazon.com/news/retail/when-is-amazon-cyber-monday-faq). (E1)
Amazon Japan gives its Black Friday dates on aboutamazon.jp's "Amazon ブラックフライデー 2026" (https://www.aboutamazon.jp/news/retail/amazon-black-friday-2026). (E1)
As read on 2026-11-09, that page had not announced them. (E1)
Amazon gives Black Friday Week's UK and German dates in UK and German releases; the Amazon Press Center DE archive (https://press.aboutamazon.com/press-release-archive?f0=de-DE) is an index of the German releases, and About Amazon UK's retail news (https://www.aboutamazon.co.uk/news/retail) an index of the UK's event news. (E2)
As read on 2026-11-09, neither index listed a 2026 announcement of those dates. (E2)

## Regional calendars

Amazon's spring event runs under its own name and dates in the UK ("Spring Deal Days") and Germany ("Frühlingsangebote"), announced in releases indexed on About Amazon UK's retail news (https://www.aboutamazon.co.uk/news/retail) and in the Amazon Press Center DE archive (https://press.aboutamazon.com/press-release-archive?f0=de-DE), and as the Big Spring Sale in the US, on aboutamazon's US "Big Spring Sale FAQ" (https://www.aboutamazon.com/news/retail/amazon-big-spring-sale-faq). (E2)
India's Great Indian Festival runs for weeks across the festival calendar, and aboutamazon.in's "Amazon Great Indian Festival" (https://www.aboutamazon.in/amazon-great-indian-festival) gives its dates. (E2)
