# Deal creative and claims

## Deal badges in ads

Responsive eCommerce Creative renders a deal badge automatically when the product qualifies for Deal of the Day, a Lightning Deal or a Best Deal, and a BFCM badge at BFCM, only on the "Shop now" variation. (E12)
Its custom headline may not contain a price or deal. (E12)
A Sponsored Brands product collection featuring products on deal automatically shows a deals call-to-action and a seasonal lifestyle image. (E12)
Since August 2026 a Product Collection create makes a Manual Collection ad. (E12)
Amazon dates the Product Collection format's end twice: create errors after January 2027, and "Beginning February 2027" in a release note. (E12)

## Ad policy on deals

An advertised deal must be live whenever the ad is: Amazon allows no deal teasing. (E13)
The price or saving appears in the same format on ad and landing page, and promotional claims match the landing page for the whole campaign. (E13)
Numeric prices or savings are allowed only where Amazon renders them dynamically, and pricing superlatives are prohibited. (E13)
"Up to X%" needs at least 10% of the promoted ASINs at or above X%. (E13)
A claim made inaccurate by dynamic pricing requires pausing the creative and changing it to a qualified claim. (E13)
An event name in an ad is bounded by the event's duration: "available on Black Friday only" means 24 hours (November 27 in 2026), "Black Friday Week" about 7 days. (E1, E13)
Sponsored ads may not mention Prime Day except through dynamically pulled detail-page information such as deal badges, and may not use "Prime" outside "Prime Day". (E13)
Ad products and ad policy at large: ../ads-launch/references/products-and-policy.md.

## Brand Store

In a Brand Store, promotional messaging is allowed only with the Featured Deals widget (at least four ASINs). (E13)
Sales-event content in a Store may start no more than seven days before the event and is removed within 24 hours after, and a Store version is submitted at least 48 hours ahead. (E13)
Store pages may not name Amazon events or numerical discounts. (E13)

## Reference prices in Europe

German law (PAngV § 11) requires an announced price reduction to state the lowest price of the prior 30 days. (E23)
The EU Court of Justice held, in 2024, that a reduction announced as a percentage or as an advantageous price must be computed from that 30-day low. (E23)
Amazon's French, Spanish, Italian, Belgian, Swedish and Polish stores display that 30-day low for price-reduction announcements and give the "Was Price" as a 90-day median excluding promotional prices. (E23)
Amazon.co.uk shows "Lowest price in 30 Days" when the price is at or below the past 30 days' lowest featured-offer price. (E23)
Amazon.de's page says event badges such as Black Friday can be added to an already active deal, and carries no 30-day sentence. (E23)
The Munich I Regional Court ruled Amazon's October 2024 "Prime Deal Days" price advertising unlawful where discounts were computed from the RRP or a customer average price, which Amazon said it would appeal, as heise online reported on July 16, 2025. (E23)
