# Who may advertise

## Sellers and Sponsored Products

- Sponsored Products needs a Professional plan or a vendor account and an eligible category. (L1)
- Adult, used, refurbished and closed-category products are excluded from Sponsored Products. (L1)
- Variation parents and suppressed listings cannot be advertised; only the child variation is eligible. (L1)

## Brand Registry

- Sponsored Brands, display ads, Streaming TV and Stores need Brand Registry or a vendor account. (L2)
- Streaming TV is not open to Health and Personal Care advertisers. (L2)
- Brand Registry needs the brand name on the product or packaging and, in one Amazon page's words, a pending or registered trademark, in another's an active registered trademark. (L2)

## Vendors

- Amazon defines a vendor as a business that sells its items directly to Amazon, which then sells them to customers. (L22)
- Amazon's FAQ lists the vendors eligible for Sponsored Products, any one criterion sufficing: Hardlines, Softlines, Supplier Express (Vendor Express) vendors with a confirmed purchase order or direct fulfillment order, and Media, Books and Consumables vendors. (L22)
- The same FAQ requires Sponsored Products listings to be eligible for the Featured Offer. (L22)
- It lists Hardlines, Softlines, Media, Books and Consumables (non-Pantry/Fresh) vendors for Sponsored Brands. (L22)
- Amazon states that only sellers must enroll in Brand Registry to advertise and vendors need not, while the SP-API Brand Analytics reports require Brand Registry for vendors and sellers alike. (L22)

## Linking and permissions

- An advertising account can be linked to only one Vendor Central account, through Access and Settings > Selling Accounts. (L23)
- The vendor approves a link request by signing in with Vendor Central credentials; a user not eligible to approve forwards it to someone with admin privileges on the Vendor Central account. (L23)
- Vendor permissions in the advertising console come in three default tiers, Viewer, Editor and Admin, while seller permissions are managed in Seller Central. (L23)
