# New-product ranking and launch evidence

## The "honeymoon"

- No Amazon page claims a "honeymoon" ranking boost for new products in Amazon's own voice. (L13)
- Amazon's cold-start patents give example durations such as ninety days; these are examples, not operating figures. (L13)
- Practitioners disagree: My Amazon Guy (Steven Pope, 2021) writes "Typically 15 – 30 days", m19 "a couple of weeks… not a hardcoded duration", Best at Amazon calls it a "myth", Code3 "debunks" it, and Canopy (Chuck Kessler) writes "Amazon has never confirmed one". (L13)

## Cold-start research

- Amazon researchers (Han et al., CIKM 2022) report that new items "typically cross the cold start barrier through very specific queries". (L14)
- The same researchers report that a prior from similar items with early stopping raised new-product impressions and purchases in an online A/B test. (L14)
- Amazon-affiliated researchers (Dong et al., arXiv 2602.14502) report that a ranking model lending new items behavioural signals borrowed from their substitutes has served customers since 2025, on a platform the paper names only as "a large E-commerce platform". (L14)

## Amazon's launch multiples

- Amazon's launch multiples (for example 36× and 231×) compare groups of new and not-new advertised products on ad-attributed outcomes, from internal data. (L17)
- Amazon links launch advertising to organic visibility only in hedged terms. (L17)

## Field research on advertising

- In field research (Dai, Kim and Luca), advertising raised Yelp businesses' purchase-intention outcomes by 7–19% and reviews by 5%, effects that disappeared once the ads stopped. (L21)
- On Zomato (Sahni and Nair), labelling a paid listing as an ad raised calls to the restaurant by 77% against the same listing unlabelled, and the ads' incremental conversions came mostly through the advertiser's organic listing. (L21)
- On Amazon (He, Hollenbeck and Proserpio), products that bought fake reviews saw a short-lived rise in rating and sales, with keyword search position not reversing within two months, while their sellers raised sponsored listings and cut prices. (L21)
