# New products and launch programs

## Reviews and ad eligibility

- Amazon advises that an advertised product have five or more reviews at 3.5 stars; that advice is guidance, and no eligibility reason reads reviews. (L3)

## Finding a new ASIN

- Product Selector's product metadata read returns `createdDate`, the date the item was first available on Amazon. (L4)
- The recommendation API groups unadvertised, recently added ASINs as `NEW_ASIN`. (L4)

## Amazon's 30-day roadmap

- Amazon's 30-day roadmap puts active listings with stock, Sponsored Products campaigns and coupons on day one. (L9)

## New-seller credits and promotions

- A seller new to Amazon earns Sponsored Products credits by creating a first SP campaign within 90 days of listing the first buyable ASIN and spending within 30 days of that campaign's launch. (L5)
- Under the 2024 terms the credit is issued within 14 days after that window, is valid 30 days and cannot pay for the spend that earned it. (L5)
- The beta v1 promotion offers and promotion rewards queries list an account's promotion offers and rewards. (L6)
- Promotional credits apply only to future costs, may be limited to one ad product and expire under their terms. (L6)

## FBA New Selection

- FBA New Selection, on Amazon's page dated Jan 14, 2026, enrols a new seller automatically when the first shipment is created within 90 days of listing the first buyable product. (L18)
- PPC Land reports revised terms from July 30, 2026 that no Amazon page read confirms. (L18)

## Vine

- Vine needs a Professional plan, FBA offers and a Brand Representative or Reseller role on a Brand Registry brand or generic products. (L7)
- Vine gives up to 30 reviews per parent and takes an existing product only with fewer than 30 reviews. (L7)
- Amazon advises enrolling in Vine with stock at least three weeks before the planned launch. (L7)

## Review Requests

- Review Requests is a Sponsored Products campaign with goal `ENGAGEMENT`, a goal fixed once set; the marketplaces where it runs, and its stage, are on Amazon's *Review Requests overview* (https://advertising.amazon.com/API/docs/en-us/guides/review-requests/overview). (L15)
- It is billed per rating or review left on the ad card, serves products with fewer than 1,000 reviews and pauses at 1,000. (L15)
- Amazon frames Review Requests as an answer to new products' cold-start problem. (L15)

## Coupons

- Under Seller Central's *Coupon eligibility criteria* (GJHCPEFJ5JJQD52D) as captured in version 14 on 2026-10-14, a capture that names no marketplace, a product with no reviews is not barred by the rating rule, whose floors apply to products with 1–4 reviews (2.5 stars) and 5 or more reviews (3 stars). (L8)
- Amazon's full new-product guide says "Coupon quality validation rules do not apply to new products". (L8)

## New & Notable and newness messaging

- New & Notable marks products new to market in the last 12 months, subject to eligibility. (L18)
- Amazon reports at least 22% higher click-through for ads that message newness. (L18)

## Rank manipulation

- Amazon's Seller Code of Conduct, as clarified on the Seller Forums, prohibits search-rank manipulation by any name, including search-find-buy and two-step URLs. (L16)
