cx finds why spend or sales moved in your Sponsored Products, Brands and Display ads and proposes changes backed by figures. Later, from Amazon's change history, it tells if a change worked, did not, or cannot be told yet.

Some things you can ask:

- Why did my ad spend rise last week while sales stayed flat?
- Why did B0EXAMPLE1's ads suddenly stop getting impressions?
- Which campaigns keep running out of budget, and how much should I add?

To have cx review your ads every week, also install [Scheduled Tasks](https://www.npmjs.com/package/@comchatx/cx-schedule).

## What it uses

- **[Amazon Ads API](https://www.npmjs.com/package/@comchatx/cx-amazon-ads):** your Amazon advertising accounts, connected on the Amazon Ads API plugin. cx reads your campaigns and reports there, and changes your bids, budgets and targets there.
- **[Amazon SP-API](https://www.npmjs.com/package/cx-amazon-sp-api),** where you have it: your Buy&nbsp;Box, stock, fees and total sales, so cx can set your ads against your whole store.
- **[Keepa API](https://www.npmjs.com/package/cx-keepa),** where you have it: Buy&nbsp;Box and rank history.
